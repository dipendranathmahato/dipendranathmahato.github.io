Personal Bio

Welcome to my Personal Website.

Hi... I am Dipendranath Mahato (দীপেন্দ্রনাথ মাহাতো), aka. DIPEN.

Current Appointment: Lecturer at University of Texas Rio Grande Valley, Edinburg, Texas.

I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at Tulane University under the supervision of Prof. Tài Huy Hà. My research is broadly situated in commutative algebra, algebraic geometry, and combinatorial algebra.

Research Interests

  • Symbolic and Rational Symbolic Powers of Ideals
  • Fat-point Schemes & Interpolation
  • Invariants of Ideals
  • Neural Ideals
  • Matroid Association to Commutative Algebra
  • Algebraic Coding with Commutative Algebra

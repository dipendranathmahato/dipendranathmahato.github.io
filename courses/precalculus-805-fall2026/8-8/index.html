Where we are coming from

In 8.2 · Non-right Triangles: Law of Cosines, the Law of Cosines solves SAS and SSS triangles, and Heron's formula gives the area.

A wind of 20 mph from the west and a wind of 20 mph from the north are not the same, even though their sizes match. Quantities like velocity and force need both a magnitude and a direction. A vector captures both as an arrow, and in components ⟨a, b⟩ we can add vectors, stretch them, measure them, and find the angle between them.

Learning objectives View vectors geometrically; find magnitude and direction; perform vector addition and scalar multiplication; find the component form of a vector; find the unit vector in the direction of v; perform operations with vectors in terms of i and j; find the dot product of two vectors.

01 · Vectors and position vectors

Definitions
  • A vector is a directed line segment from an initial point to a terminal point. The vector from P to Q is written PQ⃗ or with a bold letter such as v.
  • Equal vectors have the same magnitude and the same direction, wherever they are drawn.
  • A position vector starts at (0, 0) and ends at (a, b); it is written ⟨a, b⟩.
  • The vector from C(x₁, y₁) to D(x₂, y₂) equals the position vector ⟨x₂ − x₁, y₂ − y₁⟩.

02 · Magnitude and direction

For v = ⟨a, b⟩
  • Magnitude: |v| = √(a² + b²).
  • Direction (measured counterclockwise from the positive x-axis): θ = tan⁻¹(b/a) in Quadrant I; tan⁻¹(b/a) + 180° in Quadrants II and III; tan⁻¹(b/a) + 360° in Quadrant IV.

Always sketch the vector first: the calculator's tan⁻¹ only returns angles between −90° and 90°.

03 · Adding, subtracting, and scaling vectors

Operations with u = ⟨a, b⟩ and v = ⟨c, d⟩
sumu + v = ⟨a + c, b + d⟩: place v's tail at u's tip (tip-to-tail).
differenceu − v = u + (−v) = ⟨a − c, b − d⟩, where −v = ⟨−c, −d⟩ has the same length and the opposite direction.
scalar multiplekv = ⟨kc, kd⟩: |kv| = |k||v|; same direction if k > 0, opposite if k < 0.
i and j formi = ⟨1, 0⟩, j = ⟨0, 1⟩, so ⟨a, b⟩ = ai + bj and the operations act on the coefficients.

04 · Unit vectors and magnitude–direction form

Definitions
  • A unit vector has magnitude 1. The unit vector in the direction of v ≠ 0 is u = v / |v|.
  • A vector with magnitude |v| and direction θ is v = |v|(cos θ i + sin θ j).

05 · The dot product and the angle between vectors

Dot product
  • v · w = ac + bd for v = ⟨a, b⟩ and w = ⟨c, d⟩. The result is a scalar (a number), not a vector.
  • The angle between v and w: θ = cos⁻¹( (v · w) / (|v||w|) ).
  • v and w are perpendicular (θ = 90°) exactly when v · w = 0.

  1. Find the magnitude and direction of the vector from P(3, 7) to Q(1, 1).

    Show answer

    v = ⟨1 − 3, 1 − 7⟩ = ⟨−2, −6⟩. |v| = √40 = 2√10. Quadrant III: θ = tan⁻¹(3) + 180° ≈ 251.6°.

  2. Given v = ⟨−4, 4⟩ and w = ⟨6, −5⟩, find 3v − 2w.

    Show answer

    3v = ⟨−12, 12⟩ and 2w = ⟨12, −10⟩, so 3v − 2w = ⟨−24, 22⟩ = −24i + 22j.

  3. Find the angle between v = ⟨−2, −5⟩ and w = ⟨9, −1⟩.

    Show answer

    v · w = −18 + 5 = −13; |v| = √29 and |w| = √82. θ = cos⁻¹(−13/(√29 · √82)) ≈ 105.5°.

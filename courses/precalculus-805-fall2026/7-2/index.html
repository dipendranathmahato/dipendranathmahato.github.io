Where we are coming from

In 7.1 · Solving Trigonometric Equations with Identities, we verified identities by rewriting one side until it matched the other.

We know exact values for multiples of 30° and 45°. Angles such as 15°, 75°, 105°, or 13π/12 are not on that list, but each one is a sum or difference of two special angles: 75° = 45° + 30°, 15° = 45° − 30°, 13π/12 = 5π/4 − π/6. The sum and difference identities turn those decompositions into exact values.

Learning objectives Use the sum and difference formulas for cosine, sine, and tangent; use sum and difference formulas for cofunctions.

01 · The six formulas

Sum and difference identities
cosinecos(α + β) = cos α cos β − sin α sin β
cos(α − β) = cos α cos β + sin α sin β
sinesin(α + β) = sin α cos β + cos α sin β
sin(α − β) = sin α cos β − cos α sin β
tangenttan(α + β) = (tan α + tan β)/(1 − tan α tan β)
tan(α − β) = (tan α − tan β)/(1 + tan α tan β)

Watch the signs: the cosine formulas switch the sign (+ becomes −), while the sine formulas keep it. In the tangent formulas, the denominator has the opposite sign of the numerator.

COMMON MISTAKES
  • cos(α + β) ≠ cos α + cos β. Check: cos(30° + 60°) = cos 90° = 0, but cos 30° + cos 60° = (√3 + 1)/2.
  • Rationalize at the end. For tan 105° = (1 + √3)/(1 − √3), multiply numerator and denominator by 1 + √3 to get −(2 + √3).

03 · When α and β are given by a ratio and a quadrant

If you know one trigonometric value of α and its quadrant, a reference triangle gives sin α and cos α (with the right signs). Do the same for β, then apply the formulas. Default: cos α = 2/5 with 3π/2 < α < 2π and cot β = 4/3 with π < β < 3π/2.

Check your understanding · Sum and Difference Identities

Answer each question on paper first, then open the answer to check yourself.

  1. Use a sum formula to find cos(23π/12) exactly.

    Show answer

    23π/12 = 5π/3 + π/4. cos(5π/3)cos(π/4) − sin(5π/3)sin(π/4) = (1/2)(√2/2) − (−√3/2)(√2/2) = (√2 + √6)/4.

  2. Given sin α = 3/5 with π/2 < α < π and tan β = 7/2 with 0 < β < π/2, find sin(α + β) and cos(α − β).

    Show answer

    cos α = −4/5; r = √53 for β, so sin β = 7√53/53 and cos β = 2√53/53. sin(α + β) = −22√53/265 and cos(α − β) = 13√53/265.

  3. Use a difference formula to find tan 75° exactly.

    Show answer

    75° = 120° − 45°: (tan 120° − tan 45°)/(1 + tan 120° tan 45°) = (−√3 − 1)/(1 − √3) = 2 + √3.

Where we are coming from

In 7.1 · Systems of Linear Equations: Two Variables, two linear equations can meet at one point, never meet, or be the same line.

A quadratic function f(x) = ax² + bx + c (a ≠ 0) graphs as a parabola. Every parabola turns at a vertex, is symmetric about a vertical axis of symmetry, and has a highest or lowest output there. The shifts and stretches from Section 3.5 describe it exactly.

Learning objectives Recognize characteristics of parabolas; understand how the graph of a parabola is related to its quadratic function; determine a quadratic function’s minimum or maximum value; convert between general and standard form.

01 · Anatomy of a parabola: general form

General form · f(x) = ax² + bx + c

If a > 0 the parabola opens up and the vertex is a minimum; if a < 0 it opens down and the vertex is a maximum. The axis of symmetry is x = −b/(2a), the vertex is (−b/(2a), f(−b/(2a))), the y-intercept is f(0) = c, and the x-intercepts come from the quadratic formula x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac))/(2a).

02 · Standard form: f(x) = a(x − h)² + k

Standard form

In f(x) = a(x − h)² + k the vertex is (h, k). Compared with y = x²: h is a horizontal shift, k is a vertical shift, |a| stretches or compresses vertically, and a < 0 reflects over the x-axis. Remember h = −b/(2a) and k = f(h).

03 · How many x-intercepts?

A parabola always has exactly one y-intercept (x = 0 is always in the domain). It can have zero, one, or two x-intercepts, also called zeros or roots. The sign of the discriminant b² − 4ac decides which.

05 · Maximum value in context: a ball in the air

A ball is thrown upward; its height in feet after t seconds is h(t) = −16t² + 64t + 3. Since a = −16 < 0, the vertex gives the maximum height.

WRAP-UP

Check your understanding · Quadratic Functions

Answer each question on paper first, then open the answer to check yourself.

  1. How do you find the vertex of f(x) = ax² + bx + c?

    Show answer

    h = −b/(2a) and k = f(h). The vertex is (h, k), and the axis of symmetry is x = h.

  2. Find the vertex, axis of symmetry, and range of f(x) = 2x² − 8x + 3.

    Show answer

    h = −(−8)/(2·2) = 2 and k = f(2) = 8 − 16 + 3 = −5. Vertex (2, −5), axis x = 2. Since a = 2 > 0, the range is [−5, ∞).

  3. How many x-intercepts can a parabola have, and how many y-intercepts?

    Show answer

    Zero, one, or two x-intercepts (depending on whether b² − 4ac is negative, zero, or positive), and exactly one y-intercept.

  4. Write g(x) = 4(x − 3)² − 7 in general form and give its minimum value.

    Show answer

    4(x² − 6x + 9) − 7 = 4x² − 24x + 29. Since a = 4 > 0, the minimum value is −7, at x = 3.

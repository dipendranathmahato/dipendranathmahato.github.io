Attendance & participation

Regular attendance and active participation are expected. Students participate in class examples, Think–Pair–Share exercises, and online Peer-Led Team Learning (PLTL) sessions.

Class activities · 10%Attend lectures, take notes, solve examples, and work with classmates on Think–Pair–Share tasks.
PLTL · 10%Participate in the scheduled online PLTL sessions and complete the worksheets with peers.
Homework · 25%Complete online ALEKS homework. The syllabus allows two attempts per homework assignment.
Reviews · 5%Four paper reviews: one before each chapter test and one before the final examination.
Learning through participation

Think individually, discuss with a partner or group, and explain your mathematical reasoning. Follow the official syllabus for make-up and attendance requirements.

Learning objectives

Learn to simplify expressions; solve equations, inequalities, and systems; identify functions, domains, and ranges; use transformations and inverse functions; and graph polynomial, rational, exponential, and logarithmic functions.

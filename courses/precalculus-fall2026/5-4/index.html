Where we are coming from

In 5.3 · The Other Trigonometric Functions, tan, cot, sec, and csc are built from x and y, and the fundamental identities connect all six functions.

On a circle of radius r, the point at angle t is (x, y) with cos t = x/r, sin t = y/r, and tan t = y/x. Drop a perpendicular from the point to the x-axis and those three numbers become ratios of the sides of a right triangle. That lets us measure heights and distances we cannot reach with a tape measure.

Learning objectives Use right triangles to evaluate trigonometric functions; find function values for 30° (π/6), 45° (π/4), and 60° (π/3); use cofunctions of complementary angles; use right-triangle trigonometry to solve applied problems.

01 · Opposite, adjacent, hypotenuse

Right-triangle ratios for an acute angle t
sinesin t = opposite / hypotenuse
cosinecos t = adjacent / hypotenuse
tangenttan t = opposite / adjacent

The reciprocals are csc t = hyp/opp, sec t = hyp/adj, and cot t = adj/opp. The hypotenuse is always across from the right angle; “opposite” and “adjacent” depend on which acute angle you stand at. The Pythagorean Theorem a² + b² = c² connects the sides, and the acute angles are complementary: α + β = 90°.

03 · Cofunctions of complementary angles

Cofunction identities

Two angles are complementary if their sum is 90° (π/2). The complement of t is π/2 − t, and the side opposite one acute angle is adjacent to the other, so

sine · cosinecos t = sin(π/2 − t)
sin t = cos(π/2 − t)
tangent · cotangenttan t = cot(π/2 − t)
cot t = tan(π/2 − t)
secant · cosecantsec t = csc(π/2 − t)
csc t = sec(π/2 − t)

04 · Angles of elevation and depression

Definitions

The angle of elevation is the angle between the horizontal line from the observer's eye and the line of sight to an object above the observer. The angle of depression is the angle between the horizontal line and the line of sight to an object below the observer.

INTERACTIVE · THREE APPLIED PROBLEMS

Choose a preset situation, change the numbers, and decide which ratio connects the known side to the unknown side.

COMMON MISTAKES
  • Degree mode: sin 40 in radian mode is about 0.745, not 0.643.
  • The angle of depression is measured from the horizontal, not from the vertical tree or building. By alternate interior angles it equals the angle of elevation from the object.

Check your understanding · Right Triangle Trigonometry

Answer each question on paper first, then open the answer to check yourself.

  1. A right triangle has legs 8 and 10; α is the acute angle opposite the side 10 and β is the other acute angle. Find sin α, cos β, tan α, cot β, sec α, and csc β.

    Show answer

    Hypotenuse = √(64 + 100) = 2√41. sin α = cos β = 10/(2√41) = 5√41/41; tan α = cot β = 10/8 = 5/4; sec α = csc β = 2√41/8 = √41/4.

  2. The angle of elevation to the top of a building is 27° from a point on the ground 140 ft from its base. Find the height to one decimal place.

    Show answer

    tan 27° = h/140, so h = 140 tan 27° ≈ 71.3 ft.

  3. A hawk on top of a 25-ft tree sees a rat on the ground with an angle of depression of 34°. How far is the hawk from the rat?

    Show answer

    sin 34° = 25/d, so d = 25/sin 34° ≈ 44.7 ft.

Where we are coming from

In 6.2 Parts I–II · Graphs of Tangent and Cotangent, tangent and cotangent have period π/|B| and vertical asymptotes where they divide by zero.

Secant and cosecant are reciprocals: sec x = 1/cos x and csc x = 1/sin x. So we graph them from their guide curves. Where the guide crosses its midline the reciprocal has a vertical asymptote, where the guide reaches a maximum or minimum the reciprocal touches it and turns around, and every output stays at least |A| away from the midline.

Learning objectives Analyze the graphs of y = sec x and y = csc x; graph variations of y = sec x and y = csc x.

01 · Secant from the cosine guide

Features of y = sec x and y = A sec(Bx − C) + D
  • sec x = 1/cos x is even. The zeros of cos x, the odd multiples of π/2, are the vertical asymptotes of sec x. Domain: all real numbers except the odd multiples of π/2. Range: (−∞, −1] ∪ [1, ∞).
  • For y = A sec(Bx): stretch factor |A|, period P = 2π/|B|, asymptotes x = (π/(2|B|))k for odd k, range (−∞, −|A|] ∪ [|A|, ∞).
  • With a phase shift and a vertical shift: asymptotes x = (π/(2|B|))k + C/B (k odd) and range (−∞, −|A| + D] ∪ [|A| + D, ∞). Neither shift changes the period.

02 · Cosecant from the sine guide

Features of y = csc x and y = A csc(Bx − C) + D
  • csc x = 1/sin x is odd. The zeros of sin x, the integer multiples of π, are its vertical asymptotes. Domain: all real numbers except kπ. Range: (−∞, −1] ∪ [1, ∞).
  • For y = A csc(Bx): period P = 2π/|B|, asymptotes x = (π/|B|)k for every integer k, range (−∞, −|A|] ∪ [|A|, ∞).
  • With shifts: asymptotes x = (π/|B|)k + C/B and range (−∞, −|A| + D] ∪ [|A| + D, ∞).

COMMON MISTAKES
  • The range is never [−|A| + D, |A| + D]. That is the range of the guide. Secant and cosecant live outside that band.
  • Secant asymptotes use odd k only (zeros of cosine); cosecant asymptotes use every integer k (zeros of sine).

WRAP-UP

Check your understanding · Graphs of Secant and Cosecant

Answer each question on paper first, then open the answer to check yourself.

  1. For y = −3 sec(πx/3), find the period, the asymptotes, and the range.

    Show answer

    P = 2π/(π/3) = 6. Asymptotes x = (π/(2 · π/3))k = (3/2)k for odd k: x = ±3/2, ±9/2, …. Range (−∞, −3] ∪ [3, ∞).

  2. For y = (1/3) sec(3x − 2π) + 2, find the period, the phase shift, the asymptotes, and the range.

    Show answer

    P = 2π/3; C/B = 2π/3 (right). Asymptotes x = (π/6)k + 2π/3 for odd k, for example 5π/6, 7π/6, 3π/2. Range (−∞, 5/3] ∪ [7/3, ∞).

  3. For y = 5 csc(x/6 + 4π/3) − 2, find the period, the phase shift, and the asymptotes.

    Show answer

    B = 1/6 and C = −4π/3. P = 12π; C/B = −8π (left 8π). Asymptotes x = 6πk − 8π, for example −8π, −2π, 4π. Range (−∞, −7] ∪ [3, ∞).

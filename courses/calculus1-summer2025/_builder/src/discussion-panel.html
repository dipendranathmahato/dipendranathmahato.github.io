Key point of this course

Module discussions on Canvas

Every module in this online summer course had its own discussion forum on Canvas, set up to keep everyone active: each student asks at least one question and helps answer a classmate’s question.

  1. 1
    Ask

    Submit at least one question related to the module content: a concept you are struggling with, a real-world example, or a curiosity you would like to explore further.

  2. 2
    Answer

    Answer at least one question posted by your peers. Meaningful contributions that show effort, clarity, and correctness are valued.

  3. 3
    Earn

    The discussions count toward your participation grade (Attendance and Discussion Participation, recorded for each module, 10% of the course grade). Additional bonus points may be awarded for exceptional explanations or creative examples.

Remember: asking insightful questions and helping others clarify their doubts is a sign of strong conceptual understanding.

02 / Areas of inquiry

Research

My work studies ideals through algebraic, geometric, and combinatorial perspectives. The central themes are interpolation and symbolic powers, with current explorations in graphs, neural codes, matroids, and algebraic coding.
01

Symbolic & rational symbolic powers

Containment problems, integral closure, rational extensions, resurgence, and asymptotic invariants.

02

Fat-point schemes & interpolation

Projective and multiprojective points; Waldschmidt-type bounds and Demailly's conjecture.

03

Combinatorial algebra

Neural ideals, binomial edge ideals, and matroid connections to commutative algebra.

04

Algebraic coding & invariants

Ideals and their algebraic invariants, with connections to algebraic coding theory.
